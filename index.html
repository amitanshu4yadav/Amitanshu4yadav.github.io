About

Solo, by design.

I work alone across every layer of a product — because I'd rather ship something complete than hand off something half-built.

Most of my work runs on InfinityFree shared hosting, which sounds like a limitation until you treat it as a constraint worth designing around: no prepared statements, no persistent connections, no shortcuts. It forces cleaner decisions.

My products are built for Indian students and everyday consumers — but held to a bar set by the interfaces they already trust: Google, Flipkart, WhatsApp, X. Premium shouldn't require a big team.
